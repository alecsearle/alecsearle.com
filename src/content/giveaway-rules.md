The Giveaway is intended to be open to legal residents of the following locations, subject to confirmation of applicable local requirements before launch:

{{ELIGIBLE_COUNTRIES}}

Two (2) potential winners will be selected at random from all eligible entries within seven (7) calendar days after the Giveaway Period ends.

Each eligible entry has an equal chance of being selected. The odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received.

Potential winners will be contacted directly using the email address submitted on the entry form.

Official winner notifications will be sent from:

**giveaway@alecsearle.com**

Potential winners must respond within seven (7) calendar days of the initial notification email.

Each potential winner must provide any reasonably necessary eligibility and shipping information.

The Organizer may request reasonable proof of age, identity, or residence when necessary to verify eligibility.

Canadian potential winners must correctly answer an unaided mathematical skill-testing question before being confirmed as winners.

If a potential winner does not respond within the stated period, cannot establish eligibility, declines the prize, or cannot legally receive it, an alternate potential winner may be selected at random.

Each individual may win a maximum of one prize.
